# Privacy Policy We respect our users' privacy and are committed to protecting their personal information and data. This Privacy Policy explains how handles information and content when users interact with the application. ## 1. Collection of Personal Data The application does not collect users' personal information or request it unless it is necessary for the proper functioning of the application. We do not require users to provide their names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or any other personal information that is not necessary to provide the application's core features. ## 2. No Transfer of User Data The application does not transmit users' personal files, photos, documents, or private content to external servers or third parties. Information and content are processed locally on the user's device and are not transferred outside the device through the application, unless the user chooses to use a specific feature that requires such processing. In such cases, the user will be informed in advance. ## 3. No Monitoring or Access to Private Content The application does not spy on users, monitor their personal activities, or access their files, photos, or documents beyond what is necessary for the features they actively choose to use. The application does not access files, the camera, microphone, or location unless required for a specific feature and authorized by the user. ## 4. No Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, exchange, or share users' personal data with companies, organizations, or individuals for commercial or advertising purposes. We do not allow third parties to access users' private content through the application, except when users explicitly choose to use an external service that requires the processing of certain data. ## 5. Data Retention We do not store copies of users' personal data on our servers, nor do we maintain centralized databases for storing their private content. The application may store certain information locally on the user's device when necessary to provide its features. Users remain in control of this information and can delete it through the application's features or their device settings. ## 6. Third-Party Services We do not share users' data with third-party services or external providers without a clear operational reason. If the application includes external services necessary for certain features, the nature of these services and the data they may process will be clearly explained in this Privacy Policy. ## 7. Information Security We are committed to implementing appropriate technical measures to protect the information handled by the application and prevent unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. ## 8. User Rights Users have the right to: - Use the application without providing unnecessary personal information. - Control the permissions granted to the application. - Delete data stored locally on their devices. - Stop using the application and revoke previously granted permissions at any time. ## 9. Children's Privacy The application is not designed to collect children's personal information and does not require children to provide unnecessary personal data to use its features. ## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy whenever changes are made to the application's features or data processing practices. Any updated version will be published on the official Privacy Policy webpage. We believe that privacy is a fundamental user right. We are committed to developing applications that respect this right and give users control over their personal information and private content.
